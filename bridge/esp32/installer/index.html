Install the DSPi Link bridge

Turn an ESP32 board into a network bridge for your DSPi, so DSPi Console and your browser can control it over Wi-Fi.

What you need

  • An ESP32, ESP32-S3, ESP32-C3 or ESP32-C6 board with at least 4 MB of flash.
  • Three jumper wires, and a USB cable for the ESP32 board.
  • Chrome or Edge on a computer, to install the firmware.
  • A phone, to join the bridge to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.

1Wire the board to your DSPi

Choose your board's chip to see its pins:

ESP32 board DSPi
GPIO 17 (TX) GPIO 17 (UART RX)
GPIO 18 (RX) GPIO 16 (UART TX)
GND GND

The board's TX goes to the DSPi's RX, and RX to TX. Keep the wires short, under 30 cm. You can choose other pins later in the bridge's settings.

Power the ESP32 board from its own USB socket, not from the DSPi's 3.3 V pin: Wi-Fi draws more current than the DSPi can supply. The ground wire is still needed.

2Turn on your DSPi's UART

With the DSPi plugged into your computer by USB, open DSPi Console and set its UART settings once. The DSPi remembers them.

UART control interface On
TX pin / RX pin GPIO 16 / GPIO 17
Baud rate 1,000,000
Notifications On

3Install the firmware

Plug the ESP32 board into this computer, press Install, and choose the board's port. Installing erases the board and takes about a minute.

This browser cannot reach USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Installing needs a secure page. Open this page from its https:// address.

If no port appears, try another USB cable (some carry power only), or hold the board's BOOT button while plugging it in.

4Join it to your Wi-Fi

On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network named DSPi Bridge followed by four characters. The setup page opens by itself; if it does not, open http://192.168.4.1. Choose your home network, enter its password, give the bridge a name, and choose whether to protect it with a PIN.

Only 2.4 GHz networks work; ESP32 boards cannot use 5 GHz.

5Control your DSPi

DSPi Console finds the bridge by itself. In a browser, open the address the setup page showed, such as http://dspi-bridge-1b34.local:11915. The same page has the bridge's settings and firmware updates.

Firmware files for flashing by hand, and the source: releases · the bridge. To start again from scratch, hold BOOT for 15 to 30 seconds and let go.
